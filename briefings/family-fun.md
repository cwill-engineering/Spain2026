## Sports Deep Dive (so each kid gets a moment)

### ⚽ For Elise — soccer

**Real Madrid Bernabéu Tour (Madrid, Jun 1–4)**

- **Where:** Santiago Bernabéu Stadium, Av. de Concha Espina 1
- **When we'd go:** Best on **Tue Jun 2** late morning (post-Retiro) or Wed Jun 3 morning. Tour is open Mon–Sat 9:00–19:00.
- **Why it matters:** Real Madrid won their **15th UEFA Champions League** title. **Best club of the 20th century** by FIFA. Trophy room is hilariously full.
- **The cool stuff:** 360° pitch view from the new retractable roof, walk through the tunnel, sit in the dugout, visit the trophy room with all 15 Champions League cups, Bernabéu's Hall of Fame.
- **For a 7-year-old:** there's a kid trail with stamps. Also a Realmadrid Games attraction (penalty kicks, VR shootouts) — extra ticket.
- **Tickets:** [realmadrid.com/en-US/tour-bernabeu/individual](https://www.realmadrid.com/en-US/tour-bernabeu/individual) — €35 adult, €25 child. **Book online to skip lines** (long on summer weekends).
- **Heads up:** La Liga 2025–26 ended **May 23**, so no live matches during our window. The legendary Dani Carvajal had his farewell match May 23 — Elise can look for his statue/jersey in the museum.

**Camp Nou Immersive Tour (Barcelona, May 29–Jun 1)**

- ⚠️ Stadium is **closed for renovation through at least late 2026.** Traditional tour (pitch, dressing rooms, tunnel) is NOT available.
- What IS open: **Barça Immersive Tour + Construction Viewpoint** (€36 adult, €28 child).
- **Where:** Camp Nou site, Les Corts — 25 min metro from Eixample.
- **What you see:** Digital museum with Messi's Ballons d'Or, 360° audiovisual room, observation deck overlooking the active €1.5B reconstruction, photo booth on original stadium benches.
- **Verdict:** Skip unless we have spare time. Save Camp Nou for a future trip after reopening.

**Valencia CF Mestalla walk-by (Valencia, May 27–29)**

- Historic 1923 stadium, 49,000 capacity, weathered and beloved — the opposite of Bernabéu's modernity.
- **Walk-by works fine** (we don't need to do the tour). It's on the way between the beach and Old Town.
- **Tell Elise:** Valencia CF won 6 La Liga titles, beat Bayern Munich in the 2001 Champions League final on penalties. Their orange-and-white jersey is one of the most distinctive in Europe.
- Spain's **women's national team** (La Roja Femenina) won the 2023 World Cup — many players are Valencianas or Catalanas. Look for Aitana Bonmatí (Barcelona) and Salma Paralluelo (Spain).

**Estadi Olímpic Lluís Companys (Barcelona — wedding shuttle pickup area)**

- 1992 Olympics opening ceremony venue, currently used by FC Barcelona for La Liga matches while Camp Nou is rebuilt.
- Walk-by on Montjuïc — combine with the cable car / castle hike.

### 🤼 For Carson — wrestling

**Tarragona Roman amphitheater (visible from Valencia→Barcelona train, May 29)**

- **~11:30 AM, right side window** — Roman amphitheater built into the cliff above the Mediterranean ~125 AD.
- **Held 14,000 spectators.** Gladiator fights, wrestling matches, public executions, Christian martyrdoms. The actual sand-and-stone version of WWE.
- A Visigothic church was later built into it because three bishops were executed here in 259 AD.
- **If we ever return** — Tarragona is a UNESCO World Heritage city with the best preserved Roman ruins in Spain outside Mérida.

**Greco-Roman wrestling at the Prado / Reina Sofía**

- **Goya's "Brawl on a Hillside" (Riña a garrotazos)** — Prado, Black Paintings room. Two men beating each other with clubs while sinking into mud. One of the most disturbing wrestling-adjacent images ever painted. Carson will love this.
- **Hercules cycle by Zurbarán** in the Prado — 10 paintings of the 12 labors. Includes Hercules wrestling Antaeus and the Nemean Lion.
- **Velázquez's "Mars"** — Roman god of war sitting exhausted post-fight. Genius portrait.

**Spanish wrestling traditions (talking points, no venue)**

- **Lucha leonesa (León wrestling)** — Spanish traditional grappling from León province. Belt-based, similar to schwingen. Not in our trip cities but Spain HAS a folk wrestling tradition.
- **Lucha canaria** — Canary Islands wrestling. Two men in a circle (terrero), Greco-Roman ancestor. Olympic candidate sport.
- **Greco-Roman wrestling** — Spain has Olympic medals (most recently bronze, 2024). Brand: "lucha grecorromana."
- **Pankration** — ancient Greek total-combat sport (modern MMA's ancestor). Romans loved it. Carson can hunt for pankration vase art in the Prado / archaeological museum.

**Bullfighting — adjacent (and complicated)**

- **Las Ventas** (Madrid) is the Vatican of bullfighting. Located 20 min from our Airbnb.
- **San Isidro** festival ended May 15, so no live corridas during our visit.
- Bullfighting **museum** is interesting as combat-sport history (don't have to defend the practice). Open Mon–Sun, ~€16.
- **Mestalla stadium in Valencia** has bullfighting history too (Plaza de Toros next door, 1859).

### 🏒 For Harrison — hockey

**FC Barcelona roller hockey (Hoquei Patins) — Spain's secret hockey powerhouse**

- **The biggest fact:** **FC Barcelona's roller hockey team has won the European Cup (Champions League) more than 23 times.** They are to roller hockey what Real Madrid is to football.
- **Where they play:** **Palau Blaugrana** — next door to Camp Nou. We pass it if we do the Camp Nou Immersive Tour.
- **Schedule:** OK Liga regular season ended; **playoffs are now.** Most recent home game was **Tue May 26** (yesterday) vs CE Noia Freixenet. They may host a quarterfinal/semifinal at Palau Blaugrana while we're there — check fcbarcelona.com/en/roller-hockey for late-May 30 or 31 games.
- **What it looks like:** Quad roller skates (NOT inline), small fast ball, stick like an ice hockey stick but shorter and curved. Speed of pro hockey, hands of pro lacrosse, drama of soccer dives. Spain + Portugal dominate the sport worldwide.
- **Merch:** FC Barcelona Megastore (Camp Nou site) has the Hoquei Patins kit — orange or blue. **Get Harrison the jersey** — almost no one in Tennessee will have one.

**Field hockey (Hockey hierba)**

- **RC Polo Barcelona** and **Atlètic Terrassa** are Spain's top clubs — both Catalonia-based. Spain's national field hockey team has Olympic silver (2008) and World Cup bronze.
- The **Olympic field hockey** stadium for Barcelona '92 is the **Estadi de la Foixarda** on Montjuïc. We can walk by on a Montjuïc hike.

**Ice hockey in Spain (yes, it exists)**

- **Liga Iberdrola** — Spain's professional ice hockey league. Tiny but real. Closest rink to us would be near Madrid but the season is **over** by late May.
- **Hielo CG Barcelona** — public ice rink near Eixample. Could be a sightseeing-only stop if Harrison really wants to see Spanish ice hockey.
- **Real fact for Harrison:** Spain's ice hockey nat team plays in IIHF Division II. They beat the Netherlands in 2025. Underdog story.

**Hockey-related stops worth a walk-by**

- **Palau Sant Jordi** (Montjuïc) — Olympic indoor arena, also hosts elite figure skating + roller hockey events.
- **FC Barcelona Megastore** — full roller hockey + field hockey + ice hockey kit available. The only place outside Catalonia where you'll find proper hoquei patí gear.

### 🤓 The Nerd Tier (all three kids)

**Architecture as math**

- **Sagrada Família's columns are catenary curves** turned upside down — the shape a hanging chain makes. Gaudí used this to support immense weight without flying buttresses. **Hyperboloids** form the windows.
- **City of Arts and Sciences** (Valencia, Calatrava) — bridge and dome geometry; every roof is a slice of a section of a different geometric solid.
- **La Pedrera roof chimneys** look like fantasy soldiers — actually each is a different optimized ventilation form.

**History dense enough to argue about**

- **Atocha bombing memorial** (Madrid, 2004) — 193 killed by Al-Qaeda. Three days before national election. Changed Spanish politics.
- **Plaça Sant Felip Neri** (Barcelona) — bombed by Italian fascists in 1938 during Civil War; **20 children died** in the courtyard. The bullet marks are still in the church wall. Heavy but the kids should see it.
- **Bunkers del Carmel** (Barcelona) — Civil War anti-aircraft fortifications. 360° view + history.
- **Guernica** (Madrid, Reina Sofía) — Picasso's response to the 1937 Nazi/fascist bombing of the Basque town. The single most important political painting of the 20th century.

**Science nerd**

- **CosmoCaixa** (Barcelona) — best science museum in Spain. Geological wall (real cross-section of Earth's crust), flooded Amazon ecosystem, IMAX planetarium. **Sat May 30: NAUKAS festival** "Ciencia para salvar el mundo" 10:45 AM — could be a Valerie-led activity for the non-tour folks.
- **L'Oceanogràfic** (Valencia) — Europe's largest aquarium, 45,000 animals across 500 species. Belugas, sharks, dolphins, walruses. **Maria Pilar has discount tickets — ask her.**
- **Reial Acadèmia de Ciències i Arts de Barcelona** — 1764 observatory, occasional public tours.

## Games for the Train + Down Time

**On the train**

- **Country license-plate spotting** — Spanish provinces have city codes (M=Madrid, V=Valencia, B=Barcelona, A=Alicante, T=Tarragona). First to find 10 different ones wins.
- **Cuenca to Valencia castle-spotting** — there are 7+ visible.
- **Spanish word of the day** — pick a word each morning, count overheard uses. *Vale* is too easy; try *guay* (cool), *flipar* (to freak out, positive), *molar* (to be cool).

**Phone games for downtime**

- **GeoGuessr Spain map** — drop in random Spain location, guess where you are. Free online.
- **Duolingo Spanish push** — kids race to a daily streak.
- **Google Arts & Culture "Art Selfie"** — match your face to a museum portrait. Run it at the Prado.

**Real-world games**

- **"Find the king"** — every city has a king-on-horseback. Count them.
- **"Photograph one (legal) graffiti tag per city"** — Barcelona has the best.
- **"Best vermut tap"** — adults rate every vermut on tap they encounter; winner gets the title at end of trip.
