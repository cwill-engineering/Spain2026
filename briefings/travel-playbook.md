## ✈️ Airports

### Madrid Barajas (MAD)

**Williams arrival May 27 · T4S** | **Both departures Jun 3 / Jun 4 · T4S**

- **T4S = Satellite terminal** for all non-Schengen JetBlue, Delta, Iberia long-haul. After deplaning, take the underground people-mover from T4S to T4 main (5–8 min ride, runs continuously) — passport control is in T4 main.
- **Passport control:** EU/US/UK/Canadian/AUS passports use the **automated e-gates** (look for the blue "AUTOMATED BORDER CONTROL" signs) — much faster than manual desks. Kids under 14 must use manual desks with a parent.
- **Baggage claim:** T4 main, ground level. JetBlue bags appear on carousels 18–22 typically.
- **Customs:** Green "Nothing to declare" lane if no agriculture/cash > €10k. Otherwise red lane.
- **Cercanías station:** Follow "🚆 Cercanías Renfe" signs in T4 main. Walk ~10 min from baggage to platform — it's downstairs from the main hall. **Buy ticket at machines** before going down (~€2.60 to Chamartín or Atocha).
- **Taxi:** Flat-rate **€33 to anywhere inside M-30** (which covers La Latina, Sol, Atocha). Cash or card. Stand: T4 main arrivals, exit doors marked **"Taxi"**.
- **Uber / Cabify / Bolt:** All work in Madrid; pickup at designated zones outside arrivals (a sign will say "VTC"). Expect €30–40.

**Departure side (Jun 3 / Jun 4):**
- **Be at T4S 2.5 hrs before international flight** — JetBlue check-in counters at T4S directly (rare for satellite terminals to have their own check-in, MAD is one of them).
- **Security at T4S** is its own checkpoint — no need to clear T4 main first if you're already at T4S.
- **Lounges:** Iberia Velázquez Lounge (T4 main, before customs), Sala VIP Cibeles (T4S, airside, accessible via Priority Pass).
- **Liquids:** Standard 100 ml limit. New CT scanners at some lanes allow laptops + liquids to stay in bag — follow agent direction.
- **Corpus Christi Jun 4:** Airport operates normally; downtown is impacted, not the airport.

## 🚄 Renfe (AVE / Euromed) — boarding rules

### Hard rules that get people screwed

1. **Tickets close ~2 min before departure.** Renfe locks the QR scan a couple minutes before — **be on the platform 10 min before**.
2. **Security scanner at the station entrance.** AVE / Euromed have an airport-style X-ray for bags at the platform entry. Allow 5 extra min, especially at Atocha and Sants. Smaller stations (Chamartín AVE side, Joaquín Sorolla, Valencia Nord) usually faster.
3. **One large bag + one carry-on per ticket.** Theoretical limit, rarely enforced for Premium passengers, but don't push it with 8 bags split across 6 tickets — that's fine (avg = 1.3 each).
4. **Show ticket QR + photo ID** at the scanner. Save tickets offline in the **Renfe app** before boarding.
5. **Coach numbers are on signage above the platform.** Coach 1 is usually at the **front or rear** end of the platform — check the digital display board before walking the length of the platform.

### Premium amenities (we have these on all 3 trains)

- **Free Club Lounge access** at major stations from 1 hr before departure: Chamartín, Atocha, Sants. Coffee, pastries, snacks, soft drinks, WiFi, kids area. **Use it.**
- **Meal onboard:** Almuerzo (lunch) on the 14:30 ZUBYYB and 09:06 Euromed; Cena (dinner) on the 19:40 4XYBG8 fallback.
- **Bigger seats**, free WiFi (spotty in tunnels), priority boarding.

### Station differences — DO NOT MIX UP

| City | Stations | Use for |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Madrid** | **Chamartín-Clara Campoamor** (north) | **Primary** 14:30 ZUBYYB to Valencia |
| **Madrid** | **Atocha-Almudena Grandes** (south) | **Fallback** 19:40 4XYBG8 to Valencia · Arrival Jun 1 from Barcelona |
| **Valencia** | **Joaquín Sorolla** (AVE hub, southwest) | **AVE to/from Madrid** lands here · Tram L1 to centro |
| **Valencia** | **Estació del Nord** (city center) | **Euromed to Barcelona** departs here · Beautiful Modernista building |
| **Barcelona** | **Barcelona-Sants** (only one for AVE/Euromed) | Both BCN trains in our trip |

⚠️ **Valencia has two stations.** Joaquín Sorolla = AVE; Estació del Nord = Euromed. **They're 10 min apart but not connected.** Don't show up to the wrong one.

### Bags onboard

- **Racks at each end of the coach** — board within the first 10 min of the boarding window to claim space. The big ones at the **front of Coach 1** disappear fast.
- **Overhead racks** for carry-ons (smaller than airline).
- **Coach 13 (Euromed)** is mid-train; Coach 1 (AVE) is the front car. Look at the digital board on the platform for "Coche 1" → that's the position.

### Booking tip — Coach 1 on Premium

Coach 1 on Premium AVE is the **executive coach**, often quieter and closer to the Club Lounge entrance at Chamartín/Atocha/Sants.

## 🚇 Cercanías (Madrid commuter rail)

**Used by us on May 27 (Barajas → Chamartín or Atocha).** For the full landing-to-AVE walkthrough see **🛬→🚄 May 27 PRIMARY** under Airports → Madrid Barajas above.

## 🚖 Taxi, Uber, and rideshare reality

The short answer: **Uber only really works in Madrid**. Barcelona and Valencia, default to taxis (street, parada, or FreeNow app).

| City | Uber | Cabify | FreeNow | Bolt | Default move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Madrid** | ✅ UberX, **XL**, Comfort, Black | ✅ Strong | ✅ Strong (legal taxis) | ✅ Available | **Uber XL or Cabify Grupo** |
| **Barcelona** | ⚠️ Pre-book only, very few XL | ✅ Limited fleet | ✅ **Best option** — books legal taxis | ❌ Effectively gone | **FreeNow** or walk to a parada |
| **Valencia** | ⚠️ Small fleet, 10–15 min waits | ✅ Solid | ✅ Solid | ⚠️ Limited | **Cabify** or walk to a parada |

**Why Barcelona is different:** Catalonia regulates VTC (Uber/Cabify) much harder than the rest of Spain. Uber pulled out, came back in 2021, and now operates mostly through licensed taxi partners (Uber Taxi). Uber XL is essentially nonexistent. Local black-and-yellow taxis are abundant, well-regulated, and accept cards — and **FreeNow is the same experience as Uber** (book from phone, see fare, pay in app) using those same taxis. Install FreeNow before you go.

**For 9 passengers + 8 bags, you're getting 2 cars in every city** — no single-car magic exists unless you pre-book a 9-seat private van service (Welcome Pickups, Kiwitaxi). For Madrid `Atocha → Airbnb`, just grab the front 2 taxis at the parada.

**Fixed airport fares (no surge, no haggling):**
- **Madrid Barajas ↔ anywhere inside M-30** = **€33** flat (covers La Latina, Sol, Atocha, Chamartín).
- **Barcelona El Prat ↔ within city** = **€39** + €4.20 supplement.
- Both apply 24/7. Drivers cannot legally charge more.

**XL / van bookings:**
- **Madrid:** Uber XL fits 6 + 4 bags. Cabify Grupo same. Order both apps and grab whichever shows up first.
- **Barcelona:** Skip the XL search — book 2 separate FreeNow taxis or take the 2 at the front of the parada.
- **Valencia:** Cabify Grupo is the only app that explicitly handles 6+ pax. Otherwise 2 white taxis from the rank.

**Always:**
- All taxis take cards (post-2022 mandate). Keep ~€50 cash in small bills as backup.
- Tipping is optional — round up to the nearest €1–2.
- You **cannot hail Uber/Cabify on the street**; all rideshare is pre-booked through the app.
- Taxis at official paradas can be hailed; on the street look for the green light on top of the car ("Libre" = available).
- **Apps to install before flying:** Uber, Cabify, FreeNow (Barcelona MUST), Bolt (Madrid only, optional).
